Make the play

Passing

Slingshot: pull away from your target, then release. The ball flies in the opposite direction.

Direct drag: drag toward the spot you want to throw to.

Tap receiver: tap near a receiver to snap and throw. During a live play, tap your target spot.

Lob gives the route more time. Bullet arrives faster.

Read and react

The small lead ring predicts where a receiver can arrive: green is on target, gold needs an adjustment, and white is out of reach. Coverage can still contest a pass.

Defenders crouch before a dive. Juke up or down as they commit. Jukes share a one-second cooldown.

Drag to steer after the catch. Keyboard: ↑ / ↓ to juke, Esc to pause.
