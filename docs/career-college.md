# College senior season

New careers default to college. Pro quick start remains available, and existing pro saves keep their progression.

- 32 original programs across Southern, Heartland, Atlantic and Western conferences.
- Each school plays all seven conference opponents and five distinct nonconference opponents, with six home games and six away games.
- Top two conference records qualify for each conference final. Overall wins, point differential and team ID break ties.
- Four conference champions advance to national semifinals and a final.
- User controls the senior starting QB with the existing plays and controls.
- Powerhouse / competitive / rebuilding attribute offsets: +4 / 0 / -4.
- All paths share the existing attribute ceiling of 95.
- School tier changes supporting-cast ratings; scheme strengthens relevant receiver or run-blocking positions.
- Weekly efficiency goals award 15 / 20 / 25 bonus XP by tier, with easier thresholds for rebuilding programs.
- Scouting considers completion rate, yards per attempt, touchdown and interception rates, wins, opponent defense and difficulty. Fewer than six attempts receives a low scouting grade. Quarter length does not directly add draft stock.
- The easiest difficulty used during a match is retained in its scouting context, including across reloads.
- Draft projections blend the season's game grades with a two-game neutral prior. Team strength supplies the opening pro draft order, with QB need choosing among nearby picks.
- Draft selection is persisted when revealed. Reloading does not reroll it.
- The drafted player keeps identity, attributes, XP and unspent upgrade points. College history and awards are archived separately from pro statistics.

The first version uses four fictional regional conferences inspired by college football traditions. It does not replicate current real-world memberships, rosters or rankings. Bowl games, recruiting, transfers and additional playable positions are future work.
