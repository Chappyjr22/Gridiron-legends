# College honors and draft payoff

Draft night has four in-game tabs: senior-season review, scouting and award impact, selection and team rationale, and rookie attributes/supporting cast/targets. The content scrolls independently of touch navigation and the footer. The selection is locked when first generated; reopening, reloads and legacy drafts never reroll a destination. Reports, honors, conversion and rookie targets are archived at pro entry.

## MVP

At the end of the 12-game regular season, compare qualifying QBs, RBs, WRs and TEs across the league. Require nine games and at least 54 QB attempts, 27 RB carries or 27 receiver targets. Postseason games never affect this award. No winner is invented when old saves lack qualifying box scores.

Score: 45% efficiency percentile within position group, 15% yards per game percentile, 15% touchdowns per game percentile, 15% ball security and 10% team win rate. QB efficiency combines completion rate and yards per attempt; RB efficiency uses yards per carry; receivers combine catch rate and yards per target. Receiver positions share a comparison group. Ties break by TDs per game, then stable player ID. Honors are finalized once. This is an offensive MVP because comparable individual defensive statistics are not available.

## Draft boosts

Game scouting performance remains the base projection. College MVP reduces the distance from pick 1 by 28%; a national championship then reduces the remaining distance by 16%, rounding after each step. Both stack, duplicate award entries do not stack, and pick 1 is the floor. Example: base pick 100 becomes 72 with MVP and 61 with both awards. Base pick 3 becomes 2 with both. A championship alone does not guarantee a first-round pick.

The UI records each before/after projection and distinguishes projection from the actual selection. Existing QB-need selection within five picks is preserved. An already-generated draft retains its original projection and destination. The final game recap refreshes draft stock after postseason results and awards settle.

Rookie expectations are 9/7/5 wins for early/middle/late-round picks, 60% completions and an interception rate at most 3%, with 100 attempts for the rate targets. These are saved targets, not new benching or contract rules. Existing confidence and contracts remain in place.

Automated checks cover winners, CPU MVPs, eligibility, regular-season scope, stacking, diminishing returns, duplicate protection, reloads, legacy drafts and pro archives. Mobile UI coverage uses 844x304, 667x375 and 844x390, including all tabs and pro entry. These are fixtures, not manual gameplay.
