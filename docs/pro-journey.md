# Pro Journey: Season and Coach

Player → Career Story → Pro Journey groups the existing rookie targets and coach feedback into compact Season and Coach views. College retains its Draft view.

Season uses the saved draft targets and first pro regular-season record. The record stays explicitly labeled as the rookie year after season rollover. Veterans without a recorded rookie year do not receive invented results.

Coach shows current confidence, the last game's recorded changes, the next focus, and the contract renewal outlook. New completed games store confidence before and after the result plus the existing win/loss, weekly objective, coach challenge and interception adjustments. The calculation and its 0–100 bounds are unchanged. Older results without a breakdown show no invented change.

The renewal outlook uses the existing rules: confidence at least 70 means four seasons, at least 40 means three, otherwise one. Renewal occurs when the current contract expires, so the outlook is conditional on future confidence. Confidence does not trigger benching. The saved game-nine rookie review remains available in Coach.

This pass does not add rewards, change progression, or implement the proposed year-end presentation. The named preview uses the existing isolated preview account service.
